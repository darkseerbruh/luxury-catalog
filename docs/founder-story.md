# Founder story (source of truth) — Arielle, Luxury Catalog

Captured from the owner 2026-07-05 (her own words, lightly organized). Use this as the
grounded source for the About page and any founder content. **Do not invent beyond it.**
Feature-reality notes at the bottom so pivots stay honest.

## The origin frustration (the "why")
- She needed bags **with feet** (the protective metal studs on the base). No resale site
  had a **"with feet" filter**. She often couldn't even tell from the imagery, including
  brand kits, even after deep research.
- She was in **Salt Lake City** with **no Chanel or Hermès store nearby**, so she couldn't
  go see bags in person. Buying in the **resale market**, the information on the internet
  was everything.
- Why feet matter to her: she believes it's **bad luck to put your bag on the ground** (an
  old folk belief that lines up with a modern read on bacteria). So a very specific detail
  she deeply wanted was invisible everywhere she looked. That was the spark.

## Her relationship to bags
- A **collector who sold her entire collection and started over.**
- Still feels like a **first-time buyer every single time** she buys. It's her money.
- **Didn't come from luxury.** Every purchase is a **special moment**, never routine.
- So she is both the collector and the nervous first-buyer at once (bridges the personas).

## The data conviction (her background)
- **Years in tech, especially analytics and research.** Believes in **large datasets** to
  see trends invisible at small samples.
- Durability example: one friend says a bag holds up fine, but across **500 owners**, if 50
  had a tear or a piece of hardware fall off, that durability signal matters.
- She **reads all the reviews.** A **thousands-of-dollars** purchase should come with the
  ability to look at the data and read what other owners think.
- **Kelley Blue Book** and **Amazon reviews** exist. Even **Fragrantica** exists for
  fragrance at a far smaller price point. Why not luxury bags? She thinks it's the
  **elitist attitude** around luxury.
- The mission: **democratize this data** for the everyday people who want to invest in
  fashion. "Data important to them, to us."
