## A. Full intro (Recommended)

Luxury Catalog is the reference for designer handbags: production history, authentication markers, and what each bag actually resells for, brand by brand. Think IMDb or Fragrantica, built for handbags.

We publish brand and model pages, dated resale price ranges, authentication guides, and buying advice that helps readers decide what to get and where to get it smart. They arrive mid-decision, holding a specific bag and weighing whether to spend, where to find it, and whether it holds value.

That makes them high-intent shoppers at the moment a purchase is decided. We send them onward to the retailers and resale marketplaces that carry the exact bag they came to research, with the context that makes them ready to buy. For partners, that means qualified referral traffic tied to specific models and price points, not broad lifestyle impressions.

We're a fit for resale marketplaces, authentication services, and designer and pre-loved retailers.
