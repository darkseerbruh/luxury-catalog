# State of the Resale Market: 2026-07

*Generated 2026-07-06 from the Luxury Catalog price database (57,201 price observations across 768 styles and 28 brands). All prices in USD. Every figure below is an estimate from our own observations, not an appraisal.*

## How to read this report

- **These are asking prices, not sold prices.** Unless a line says otherwise, a figure is what sellers are listing at, not what buyers paid. Fashionphile is fixed-price, so its asking closely tracks what buyers pay; other platforms are asking only.
- **Our catalog skews high and is not yet representative of the whole market.** Coverage today: 519 of 768 catalog styles (67.6%) have at least one price observation. Read these numbers as a view into premium US resale, not the global market.
- **Every figure carries its sample size (n) and date window.** Medians with small n are directional at best; we flag n below 10.
- Rows without a recorded currency come from US-marketplace captures and are treated as USD; 0 non-USD rows were excluded from the 57,201 total.

## Scope and sources

- Data: 57,201 price observations in the Luxury Catalog database as of 2026-07-06. Aggregates use the 57,201 USD rows.
- Asking, not sold: aggregate figures use listed asking prices. We do not present them as realized sale prices.
- Fashionphile: fixed-price marketplace, so asking closely tracks what buyers pay.
- TheRealReal: consignment marketplace; prices are asking.
- Vestiaire Collective: peer-to-peer marketplace; prices are asking.
- eBay: bid/ask marketplace; listed prices are asking, not realized bids.
- ebay: bid/ask marketplace; listed prices are asking, not realized bids.
- Representativeness: our catalog's price mix currently skews toward higher-priced styles and premium US resale platforms. It is not yet a representative sample of the whole resale market, and these figures should be read with that in mind.
- All figures are estimates from observed listings, not appraisals, and are dated 2026-07-06.
