# What it actually costs

Ask TikTok what a Birkin costs and you get a round number with no date, no condition, and no way to tell a guess from a sale. Price is the single biggest bag search there is, eight of the queries below clear 200K a month each, and almost all of the answers are vibes.

This series answers with data instead. Every entry shows the current asking median, the sold median where we hold sold data, the sample size, and the date. Every number is an estimate from dated comps within a condition tier, never an appraisal of your bag.

## Why ask and sold are different numbers

The gap is the whole story, and one bag shows it best. The Louis Vuitton Neverfull MM asks around $1,495 on the preloved market (n=344) but actually sells around $770 (n=87), both observed June 2026. Anyone quoting you the asking price as "what it's worth" is off by nearly half on this bag.

That is why every segment in this series separates the two, and says so when sold data is thin or missing.

## The segment format

Every bag in the series gets the same block, so readers can compare across houses:

- **Asking median:** From live listings, with n and the as-of date.
- **Sold median:** Where we hold realized sales, with n and date; marked "not yet held" otherwise, never guessed.
- **The spread:** Ask vs sold in one sentence, when both exist.
- **The hedge, always:** An estimate from dated comps, not an appraisal; condition tier stated.

## The standing rules for every entry

No number without a date and an n. No sold claim without held sold data. No blending conditions into one median. And no verdicts: We publish what the market shows and our read of it, and what a bag is worth to you stays your call.
