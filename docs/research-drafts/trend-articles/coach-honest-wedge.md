# Coach outlet names, decoded honestly

Coach runs two catalogs, mainline and outlet, and the outlet styles have their own names that the main site never mentions. That is why "erin coach bag" pulls 322K searches a month: people are holding a real Coach and cannot find it on coach.com. Nobody is being scammed, but nobody is being told the difference either.

## Why the outlet split matters for resale

An outlet style is a real Coach bag, full stop, and we do not judge anyone's catalog. The money point is different: outlet styles are made to a price and made in volume, so they resell against deep supply. Mainline styles like the Tabby carry the brand's resale ceiling; we published exactly what a Tabby sells for, sold data included, in its own piece.
