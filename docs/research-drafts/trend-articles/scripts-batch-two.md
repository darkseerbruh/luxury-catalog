# Batch two: Coach wedge + Goyard house

## COACH KIT (key: coach bags)

### 1. The preloved Coach map (~65s)

Alt opens if the default hook feels slow: "Five Coach bags, five real prices, and a thrift plan." or "Coach is the smartest door into designer bags right now. Here's the map."

Thrifting Coach or buying your first one? Here's the preloved Coach map, with real asking prices, so you know a deal when you see one. The viral one is the Coach Tabby Shoulder Bag 26, the little quilted flap all over your feed. It asks around two hundred and sixty-five dollars preloved. Its puffy sibling, the Coach Pillow Tabby, runs about two hundred and eighty-five in the 26, and the smaller 18 is the cheapest Tabby door at around two hundred. The Coach Willow Tote, the everyday carryall, asks around a hundred and sixty. The Coach Brooklyn, the slouchy shoulder bag having its moment, sits near two hundred and thirty in the 28. And the outlier is the Coach Rogue: around five hundred dollars preloved, the priciest Coach in my comps, and my take on the one that behaves most like a luxury bag on resale. Notice the pattern? Almost everything here lives under three hundred dollars. That's the whole point of Coach preloved. Every number is a live asking median from the twenty thousand plus real prices I track at Luxury Catalog dot com. I'm Arielle, and the full Coach read is on my site. Just search coach bags on the site to read more. Link in bio.

### 2. Which Tabby, and the offer move (~40s)

Want the Coach Tabby without paying feed-hype prices? Quick decode, with numbers. The classic Coach Tabby Shoulder Bag 26 asks around two hundred and sixty-five dollars preloved. The puffy Coach Pillow Tabby 26 runs a touch higher, around two hundred and eighty-five. The quiet deal is the Pillow Tabby 18: same pillow look, smaller body, around two hundred dollars. And because these mostly live on eBay, the asking price is a starting point, not a sticker: sellers expect offers, so start yours under the median and let them come to you. If they won't, the next listing is a scroll away. Numbers from my July comps at Luxury Catalog dot com; the full Coach breakdown is on the site. Just search coach bags to read more. Link in bio.

### 3. The Rogue, Coach's quiet grail (~35s)

Which Coach actually holds its price? My comps have a clear answer. Most Coach bags preloved ask between one hundred and sixty and three hundred dollars. The Coach Rogue asks around five hundred, from over a hundred listings this month, and nothing else in my Coach data comes close. My take, and it's a take: the Rogue is the Coach that resale treats like a luxury bag. So if you're thrifting and a Rogue shows up under two hundred, that's the flip. The full Coach read is on the site, search coach bags. If you own a Rogue, tell me what you paid for it in the comments.

### 4. Coach sizes price backwards (~35s)

Here's a Coach size fact that runs backwards from luxury. At Chanel, bigger doesn't cost more preloved; the maxi flap sizes ask under the medium. At Coach, bigger costs more: the Coach Brooklyn 39, the big one, asks around three hundred and thirty dollars, while the smaller Brooklyn 28 asks about two hundred and thirty. So at Coach, the small size is the value pick, and the big slouchy look carries the premium. Decide by look, but know which direction the money runs. My numbers, tracked this month at Luxury Catalog dot com; the full comparison is on the site. Just search coach bags to read more. Link in bio.

Alt opens if the default hook feels slow: "Four Goyard bags explain the whole house. Let's price them." or "Goyard has no ads and barely a website. Here's the map they don't hand you."

Curious about Goyard but lost on where to start? Here's the Goyard starter map, with real preloved prices. ### 2. The zip costs five hundred dollars (~35s)

Here's the Goyard question worth pricing before you pick: the zip. The Goyard Saint Louis is the icon, but it's open-top, and it tips over in a stroller basket or on a car seat. The Goyard Artois is the same tote language with a zip and real structure. In my comps, the Saint Louis PM asks around twenty-four hundred and sixty-five dollars and the Artois PM around twenty-nine ninety-five. So the zip and the structure cost about five hundred dollars preloved. My take: if an open top means you'd baby the bag every day, that five hundred is cheap peace of mind. The full comparison is at Luxury Catalog dot com; just search goyard bags to read more. Link in bio.

### 3. Going bigger costs thirty dollars (~30s)

A Goyard size fact you'll like: on the Goyard Saint Louis, going bigger costs almost nothing. In my comps the PM asks around twenty-four hundred and sixty-five dollars, from nearly two hundred listings, and the bigger GM asks twenty-four ninety-five, from ninety. Thirty dollars for the size jump. So pick the size your shoulder wants, not the one your budget suggests; this market isn't charging for volume. The full size read is on the site, search goyard bags.
