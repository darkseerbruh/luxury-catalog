# Who really made your bag

Every big bag has a second author. The house name is on the hardware, but a specific designer's era decided the shape, the campaign, and the resale story, and preloved buyers who know the eras shop better than buyers who only know the logo. TikTok has caught on: the eight searches below are all lineage questions, most with thin creator coverage.

Each section stands alone as a short story. Tenure dates are drafted from research notes and flagged where they still need a primary source. Prices, where they appear, are our estimates from dated comps, not appraisals.

## Louis Vuitton by Marc Jacobs

## Old Celine, meaning Phoebe Philo

"Old celine" is not an age, it is an era: Phoebe Philo's run at the house, roughly 2008 to 2018 [VERIFY: exact start and end years]. Her minimalist Luggage, Phantom, and Trapeze built a collector base loyal to the designer more than the label. The visible tell of the era's end is typographic: Hedi Slimane dropped the accent from Céline, so the accent on a label is an era marker in itself.

## How to use this when you buy

The era is a fact you can check: a date code, a label accent, a collab print, a designer's tenure window. It is often the difference between a generic vintage price and a collected one, in either direction. We hand you the markers to check, not a verdict on any single bag.
