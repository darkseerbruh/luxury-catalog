# Every Goyard bag, explained

Goyard is the house people search before they can name the bags. Twelve Goyard phrases each pull six-figure monthly searches on TikTok (Creative Center, pulled 2026-07-01), and the biggest one is simply "how much is a goyard." So this is the whole house in one place: every shape, what it actually does, and what the preloved market says.

One material note up front. The chevron print is Goyardine, a resin-coated canvas per Maison Goyard, and the trim is natural leather that darkens with wear. That patina is a feature to some buyers and a flaw to others; know which one you are before you buy.

## St Louis

**The open-top tote everyone pictures when they say "Goyard."** No zip, no structure, almost no weight. The PM carries a normal day; the GM swallows a laptop, a water bottle, and a toddler's entire afternoon. The open top is the tradeoff: light and easy, but nothing between your things and the world.

- **Preloved read:** Our comps put the St Louis PM at a median asking price around $2,455 (n=110) and the GM around $2,495 (n=45), observed 2026-06-28 to 2026-07-01. Asking, not sold, and an estimate, not an appraisal. The interesting part: on current asks, the size jump costs almost nothing.
