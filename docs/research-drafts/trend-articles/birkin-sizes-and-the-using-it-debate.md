# Birkin sizes, and whether to actually use one

Two Birkin questions dominate TikTok right now. The first is the eternal one: which size. The second is newer and more interesting: once you have one, are you allowed to actually live with it? The "using a birkin" search sits at 205K monthly with only 104 creators on it (Creative Center, pulled 2026-07-01), which means the internet is asking a question almost nobody is answering.

Sizes first, then the debate. All prices are our estimates from dated comps, not appraisals; Birkin pricing is spec-specific enough that a single headline number would be a lie.

## Getting one

The acquisition story deserves one honest, careful paragraph. Shoppers widely describe a purchase-history dynamic at Hermès boutiques, the so-called quota-bag game, where Birkin offers follow a relationship with the store. Hermès does not publish rules for this, so everything written about it is observed behavior, not policy, and experiences vary by boutique and country. The preloved market exists partly as the exit from that game: you pay the market's premium [DATA: preloved premium vs current retail, dated, by size] and skip the waiting.

## The use-it debate

Now the real question. TikTok is currently split between dinner Birkins, beach Birkins, grocery-run Birkins, and cost-per-wear girl math on one side, and the preserve-it camp on the other, keeping the bag in its dustbag between appearances.

Our take, plainly labeled as our take: use it. Jane Birkin used hers until it was covered in stickers and worn soft, and that bag is the most photographed Birkin in history. A bag that never leaves the closet is an expensive way to own anxiety.

The honest caveats, because they are real. Wear affects resale, and corner wear and handle darkening price into the comps [DATA: condition-tier spread on a benchmark size, dated]. Fashion is subjective and we don't judge the preserve-it camp; if the bag is an asset to you first, treating it like one is coherent. The mistake is only in not deciding, buying it to use and then being too scared to.
