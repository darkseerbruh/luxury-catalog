## KIT 1: Chanel in 2026

### 1. The Explainer (~50s, ~135 words)

Alt opens if the default hook feels slow: "Chanel comment sections are a mess right now, so let's clean it up." or "Three Chanel questions keep going viral. One video, all three."

If you're new to Chanel and the comment sections are a mess of numbers, here's the clean version to help you out. First thing: Chanel didn't rename anything. The classics kept their names: the Chanel Classic Flap, the Boy, the 2.55. What's new is how new lines get named. Since the Chanel 19 in 2019, each new line is named for the year it's born: the 19, the 22, and now the 25. Birth years, not sizes. And the trick is older than it looks, because the 2.55 was always a date: February 1955, when the original launched. So the Chanel 25 is simply the new everyday line from 2025. Softer and lighter than a Chanel Classic Flap, built to be carried daily, not babied, and behind nearly three million TikTok searches a month. One more 2026 headline: the oversized maxi flap from Matthieu Blazy's first Chanel collection, which creators report launched in March. And Chanel itself is just a surname, Gabrielle's. Every claim in this video checks against the twenty thousand plus real prices I track at Luxury Catalog dot com. I'm Arielle, and every bag is decoded on the site. ### 3. The Taxonomy (~45s, ~130 words)

The Chanel bags actually filling your feed, one pass. Not every bag the house has ever made, I track more than a hundred Chanel styles, but these are the ones behind the searches. The Chanel Classic Flap is the quilted one, with the chain woven through leather and the CC turn-lock. Sizes run mini, in square and rectangular, then small, then medium, which is the default people mean, then jumbo and maxi. The Chanel 2.55 is the original, named for its debut in February 1955; the tells are a rectangular Mademoiselle lock and a chain with no leather through it. The Chanel Boy is the boxier flap with the chunkier chain. The Chanel WOC means wallet on chain, and it's the price entry point to the flap family. The Chanel 19 is the puffy, big-quilted one from 2019, the first line named for its birth year. The Chanel 22 is the soft slouchy one from 2022. The Chanel 25 is the 2025 everyday line. Totes are the open-top side, currently led by the Chanel Deauville. One rule ties it all together: Since the Chanel 19 in 2019, new lines are named for the year they're born. Birth years, not sizes, and the old names aren't going anywhere. I track what every one of these actually resells for at Luxury Catalog dot com, and the full taxonomy is on the site. Just search chanel 2026 on the site to read more. Link in bio.

### 4. The Debate (~25s, ~75 words)

Is the Chanel 25 a future classic like the Chanel Classic Flap, or does it go the way of the Chanel Boy? Quick decode on that, because it matters: the Boy was THE hot Chanel of the 2010s, then the buzz moved on, and today my comps hold more preloved Boys than Classic Flaps, asking around half the medium Flap's price. That's the other path for a hot new line. Which path the 25 takes is too early to call, and anyone handing you a verdict is guessing. What I watch instead is preloved supply and asking prices in my Luxury Catalog data over the next year, because new lines start thin and the numbers fill in. My take, not a rule: Buy it because you'd carry it, not for its resale fate. The longer read is on the site, search chanel 2026. If you've already picked a side, tell me which one in the comments.
