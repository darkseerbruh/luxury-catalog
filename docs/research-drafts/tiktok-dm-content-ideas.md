## Pros / cons vs the value-first rule

Reminder of the standing rule these are judged against: start from what's useful or
surprising to the target persona, then source the asset. Never reverse-engineer a
post from a trend or an asset we happen to have.

## Recommendation

Prioritize the categories that multiply useful content rather than manufacture posts:

1. **Build the batch + b-roll + repurposing system (B4, B7, A4).** This is the
   highest-leverage move pre-launch: it makes the volume problem go away without
   compromising the value-first rule.
2. **Run Lana's Creator Search Insights method (B10) next.** It is persona-first demand
   discovery, and it plugs straight into the existing /admin/trends hub and the
   tool-name-search-demand research draft.
3. **Pilot one AI motion-design piece (A6/A7)** to test a production-speed and
   look upgrade, kept deliberately un-sloppy.
4. **Treat trending audio (category 4) as garnish**, used only when a sound genuinely
   fits a point worth making. Never build a post around a sound first.
