## 0. How to read this (and what each thing moves)

- **Popularity / Pop #** = TikTok search volume for the term. Demand.
- **Growth %** = how fast that demand is rising. `1000%+` / `41000%+` are TikTok's
  capped "breakout" buckets — read them as *"emerging, not yet crowded,"* not literal.
- **Creators / saturation** *(to be pulled)* = how many creators publish under the tag. Supply.
- **The sweet spot = high Popularity ÷ low Creators.** High demand, few people
  answering it = a gap we can own with an article + a video that points at it. This is
  the **engagement → traffic → affiliate** on-ramp (`marketing-plan.md`): every term
  that becomes a post ends at a real page with a where-to-buy / where-to-sell link.

## 5. Social strategy — what to develop, why, and the metric it moves

Filtered through: **website is the compounding asset; social is the on-ramp; every post
ends at a page with a buy/sell link** (`marketing-plan.md`). Ranked by our edge × demand ×
low saturation.

### Bet 1 — "Which luxury bag for [school / work / diaper]?" job-to-be-done series **(Recommended lead)**
- **Why:** 2.9M combined demand, thinly answered, **seasonal peak now→Sept for school**,
  and it reframes a purchase as *practical* (permission to buy). Cuts across brands, so one
  format → many posts.
- **Build:** article per job ("Best luxury tote that actually fits a laptop," "Luxury
  diaper bags, honestly ranked," "First designer bag for campus under $X") + a talking-head/
  flat-lay video pointing to it. Ground every pick in our catalog + real resale value.
- **Moves:** **engagement + affiliate clicks** (high purchase intent, direct to
  where-to-buy). Seasonal timing compounds it.

### Bet 2 — "How much does it really cost / is it worth it" price-truth series
- **Why:** 2.83M demand and the answers on TikTok are guesses. **We have real comps** —
  this is our single most defensible edge (`data-analysis-standard.md`).
- **Build:** short "the ask vs what it *sells* for" videos (Goyard, Birkin, Chanel 22,
  Celine, Jacquemus Valerie, Capucines) → the matching bag/article page.
- **Moves:** **engagement + GEO** (dense, cited comps rank in AI answers) and sets up
  **affiliate**. Frame values as **estimates from dated comps, never appraisals.**

### Bet 3 — "vs" comparison engine (feeds our `/compare` pages)
- **Why:** 2.29M demand, decision-stage intent, and we already built `/compare`.
- **Build:** mini kelly vs birkin, nano vs mini LV, Speedy 20 vs 25, new vs old Chanel,
  St Louis PM vs GM → each is a video ending on the compare page.
- **Moves:** **engagement + affiliate** (decision-stage = closest to a click).

### Bet 4 — Coach / thrift / "luxury for less" wedge (our strategic Decision 1)
- **Why:** ~1.6M thrift/resale demand + Coach breakouts (Trail 555K, tote-for-school 657K,
  cute coach 296K). Cheapest data, widest audience, most viral format.
- **Build:** thrift-haul + "what a Coach Tabby *actually* sells for" (data article already
  live) + TJ Maxx/Amazon "is it real / is it worth it."
- **Moves:** **top-of-funnel reach → traffic.** Monetize the luxury tier behind it.

### Bet 5 — Decode / "the real name of that bag" explainer (low effort, high save-rate)
- **Why:** the §1 work IS content. People search wrong names (balenciaga foil, Mombasa,
  romantique). Being the account that *names it correctly* earns saves + authority.
- **Build:** "You're calling it the wrong name" carousel/video series.
- **Moves:** **engagement (saves/shares) + GEO authority.**

### Bet 6 — Designer-lineage + archival-season "connoisseur" series
- **Why:** ~1.2M lineage demand + ~1.6M archival demand, and it is **barely answered
  cleanly** — the names, houses, and years are usually wrong on TikTok. We already own a
  **30-year seasonal naming archive** (the `archivist` agent) built for exactly this, so
  this is a rare demand × edge × low-saturation overlap.
- **Build:** "Every house [designer] designed for" timelines (Marc Jacobs → LV, Tom Ford
  → Gucci then YSL, YSL → Dior, Phoebe Philo → Céline), and "what [house] called this bag
  in [year]" archival decoders. Each ends on a bag/attribute/era page.
- **Moves:** **engagement (saves/shares on the reveal) + GEO authority** — clean, cited
  lineage/season facts are exactly what AI answers cite, and nobody else is publishing them
  tidily. Frame designer tenures as **sourced history** (dates), not opinion.

### Deprioritize for now
The mega head terms (Telfar, Miu Miu City) — huge but saturated and off our resale/data
edge; and pure hype drops we can't source honestly.
