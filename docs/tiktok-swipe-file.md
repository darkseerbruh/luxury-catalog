## 3. Sarah's weekly template (filled in for us)

Her Mon–Fri fill-in-the-blank framework, adapted to Luxury Catalog. Each is a
faceless slideshow or b-roll + text, on-voice (know-your-bags, warm, no snobbery):

- **MON — "The ___ that saves you from ___."**
  *The 30-second authentication check that saves you from a $1,200 mistake.*
- **TUE — "Unpopular opinion: ___."**
  *Unpopular opinion: the "investment bag" everyone tells you to buy first usually
  is not the one that holds value best. Here's what our data leans toward.*
- **WED — "A quick way to choose ___ when ___."**
  *A quick way to choose your first "real" bag when every review contradicts the
  last one.*
- **THU — "The part of ___ nobody talks about is ___."**
  *The part of buying preloved nobody talks about is how much a bag liner and a
  dust bag quietly change how long it lasts.*
- **FRI — "Not to be dramatic, but ___ — try ___ instead."**
  *Not to be dramatic, but "buy it for life" is doing a lot of heavy lifting — try
  "buy it, love it, resell it well" instead.*

## 5. New ideas in your style (fresh, same voice)

Grouped by the format they plug into. All faceless/photo/b-roll-able, hedged on
value (estimate, not appraisal), warm, no snobbery.

**→ "Girls will be like 'I needed this'" (trending sound + 5s clip)**
- *Girls will be like "just browsing" and it's a 40-tab deep-dive on whether the
  Speedy 25 or 30 actually fits their life.*
- *Girls will be like "it was an investment" and it genuinely was — here's the one
  that's up in resale.*
- *Boys will never know the peace of finally finding the bag that fits a laptop
  AND doesn't look like a laptop bag.*

**→ "How my brain sounds when…" (relatable-humour sound)**
- *How my brain sounds when the resale price is higher than retail and I still want
  it.*
- *How my brain sounds trying to remember if this seller's photos matched the
  hardware on a real one.*
- *How my brain sounds deciding between the one I love and the one that "holds
  value."*

**→ "Hills I will die on" (collector hot-takes)**
- *More hills: Suede is not as scary as they told you / "Vintage" is not a synonym
  for "beat up" / A good dust bag is not optional.*
- *Hill I will die on: the color everyone calls "boring" is the one you'll still
  carry in ten years.*

**→ Affirmation / "you deserve" (warm, feel-seen)**
- *You're allowed to buy the bag before you've "earned" it. Loving it is the
  reason.*
- *Reminder: a preloved bag isn't second-best. Someone chose it, carried it, and
  now it's your turn.*

**→ "I used to [hard way]… now I just [Luxury Catalog]" (product-led)**
- *I used to screenshot 12 resale listings to guess if a price was fair. Now I just
  check what the range actually is.*
- *I used to ask a Facebook group "is this real?" and hope. Now I check the markers
  myself first.*

**→ "The ___ that saves you from ___" (value / research)**
- *The 3 photos to ask every seller for that save you from a "looked great online"
  regret.*
- *The one date-code habit that saves you from overpaying for a "rare" bag that
  isn't rare.*

**→ "Which got more views?" A/B curiosity (Gracie format)**
- *Same bag, two prices, six months apart. Guess which listing sold in a day.*
- *New vs preloved of the exact same bag. Guess the price gap. (It's bigger than
  you think.)*

## 6. Caption bank — your verbatim hooks (grouped by format)

Your own lines, kept exact, deduped, sorted into the format each one is. This is
the voice reference for anything new. (Dupes noted where you sent a line twice.)

**"Netflix documentary on [mundane luxury struggle]" — deadpan meme**
- *…on having to sell my entire purse collection and start over.*
- *…on having to put my purse on my lap for the entire meal because there's no
  spare chair and it's my baby.*

> **Rules for this format (locked 2026-07-06):** it must be a genuine *struggle /
> bad thing* (documentaries are about hardship, not flexes) · **collectors don't
> purge**, so no downsizing/culling angles · **no partner/husband justification
> content** (tradwife-coded) · the sweet spot is *absurd self-inflicted devotion*
> (bag as a beloved dependent) or a *real collector hardship*. Must be
> consequential, not trivial (a small-vs-large price gap is too inconsequential).
> Approved new lines:
> - *…on holding my bag the whole flight because I don't trust the overhead bin.*
> - *…on re-buying, at triple the price, the exact bag I sold two years ago and
>   never got over.*
> - *…on being my bag's human umbrella so it stays dry while I don't.*

**"Here's exactly what I would do if I were [big purchase]" — advice/authority**
- *…buying my first big girl bag.*
- *…considering buying a bag for thousands of dollars.*

**"I wish someone had told me this before [effort]" — lesson/regret**
- *…I spent weeks researching the perfect bag.*
- *…I tried to play the Hermès game.*

**"Most people will never know [X]… but you're not most people" — insider**
- *Reminder that most people will never know whether you got it from the store or
  preloved.*
- *Most people will never know the data behind the fashion they love… But you're
  not most people.*

**Value / resale reframe**
> *Keep these casual and about ONE specific bag. No statistical hedging ("often two
> to three times," "varies by house") — it sounds robotic. The first-person,
> one-bag framing is the hedge.*

  you get it used vs new.*
- *Not to be dramatic but I'll never buy a designer bag brand new again.*
- *This is your sign to try renting designer bags instead of buying them.*

**Confessional / relatable**
- *I'm not afraid to admit that I wasted thousands of dollars on designer bags… And
  I'll probably do it again because I'm a junkie 😜*
