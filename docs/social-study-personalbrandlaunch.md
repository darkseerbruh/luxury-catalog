# Creator study: @personalbrandlaunch0 (Personal Brand Launch)

Source: TikTok profile, captured 2026-07-05 (latest 16 videos, her most recent ~5 weeks). Stats = at capture time. She runs a done-for-you short-form agency (PBL / pblaunch.com); the account IS her lead magnet. 276.4K followers, 2.5M likes.

**How to read this doc:** each video is tracked on its own. These are *her individual plays*, not an averaged philosophy. Steal the specific mechanic, not the vibe.

Legend: 👀 views · ❤️ likes · 💬 comments · 🔖 saves · ↗️ shares. Saves + shares are the reach signal; a high save:like ratio is why a video travels.

---

## Her format signature (observable, not opinion)

Every teaching video shares the same shell, so you can copy the shell exactly:

- 🎤 Talking-head, handheld mic, filmed outdoors on a deck (same spot every time = instant brand recognition).
- 🟩 Bold cover: one topic word in giant neon-lime caps over a solid outdoor frame. The cover is the whole thumbnail-sell.
- 📝 Written hook baked in at the top of frame from second one.
- 🔁 Fixed CTA outro: "Follow for more social media marketing education."
- 🧵 Recurring series names: "Weekly Diary - Episode #__", "If You Had X To Do Y, How Would You Do It?", "X vs Y".

## Per-video breakdown

### 1. POV: You Hire Me As Your Social Media Manager 😎
👀 24.5K · ❤️1,510 · 💬22 · 🔖1,205 · ↗️299 — **her breakout.** Save count ≈ like count, that ratio is the reason it traveled.
- **Format:** "POV: you hire me" walk-through, on-screen step labels, voiceover.
- **Her play:** narrates her agency's exact 6-step delivery as a numbered system: (1) Onboarding doc → kickoff call, (2) Research (find hundreds of proven viral outliers in-niche that follow the "5x rule" + IT creators on IG), (3) Ideation/scripting (15-30 scripts from proven hooks/topics), (4) Film (only step the client does, just a phone), (5) Edit, (6) Upload everywhere.
- **Why it works:** it's a "save this, this is a whole system" video. She says she *remakes this video every year*.
- **Steal:** the "POV: you hire me as your ___" framing to turn your own process into saveable content.

### 2. Using AI For Content
👀8,099 · ❤️395 · 💬4 · 🔖426 · ↗️87 — **2nd-highest saves.** Save-heavy = a keeper.
- **Format:** rapid list, "I ask AI to ___ → here's the better way I actually do it."
- **Her play, item by item:**
  - Hooks: have AI pull viral videos following the 5x rule and write out the written/verbal/visual hook to reuse.
  - Scripts: feed AI viral scripts, have it explain *why* each went viral and turn them into fill-in-the-blank templates.
  - Carousels: have AI turn your viral reels into carousels using a Canva design trained on your past branding.
  - Ideas: have AI transcribe your calls + read your calendar, then hand you a weekly list of lessons/experiences/tasks to make content from.
  - Stories: feed AI your story analytics, have it double down on your best lead-getting stories and spin variations.
- **Steal:** "AI as a research + templating assistant, you supply the real value/experience." Not "AI writes my posts."

### 3. If You Had 60 Minutes to Create a Month of Content Ideas
👀5,458
- **Format:** timed-challenge system ("if you had 60 min…"), numbered steps.
- **Her play:** (1) Account type (new vs established), (2) Pick posting load (easy/moderate/hard), (3) Niche → sub-niches → occupation, build a keyword list, (4) Research proven viral *outliers* (small/mid creators whose video beat their own average = proven, likely to repeat), (5) Ideation: build scripts around the "7 factors," start with **hook stacks**, then value → structure (educational or storytelling) → CTA (chosen by goal) → format.
- **Named frameworks:** **70/20/10 rule** for established accounts, **90/10 rule** for new accounts (content mix), credits Caleb in the content space.
- **Steal:** the outlier definition (a creator's own overperformer) and hook-stacks-first ideation.

### 4. If You Had to Make a Storytelling Video That Would Get 1M Views
👀3,733
- **Format:** blank-page-to-post system, 6 steps.
- **Her play:** (1) Pick 1 of **7 storytelling types**, (2) Use a storytelling script structure — her template-building method: find a viral storytelling video → transcribe it → turn it into a fill-in-the-blank template → save in a Google Doc, (3) Write your script line-by-line in bullets (she shows a worked example for a fictional fitness coach), (4) Pick 1 of **3 storytelling formats**: voiceover / shot-angle change / audio+B-roll+text, (5) Plan visuals (script in bullets → shots to film → editing notes for past photos/videos), (6) Film, edit, post.
- **Steal:** transcribe-a-winner-into-a-template, and "storytelling needs a wall of past B-roll to show the moment."

### 5. What Type of Content Should You Post?
👀6,354
- **Format:** decision-tree / "choose your own path" list.
- **Her play:** frames content choice as trade-off axes rather than one right answer: low-effort vs high-effort; post 7-14x/week vs polished 3-5x/week; faster results vs slower-but-meaningful; trend-based (easy planning) vs independent/creative; visual niche (need to see to learn) vs verbal niche (need to hear).
- **Steal:** the "if you want X, post this / if you want Y, post that" structure. Self-selecting, high completion.

### 6. Educational vs. Storytelling vs. Yap Reels
👀3,707
- **Format:** 3-way comparison chart, on-screen matrix.
- **Her play (the matrix):**
  - **Yaps** = hot take / opinion rant / bestie advice; lead CTA to an offer or freebie; best to turn into ads; best for new accounts; works for any niche.
  - **Educational** = follow CTA; best for saves; best for attracting clients; quickest / most beginner-friendly to make.
  - **Storytelling** = loyal-community CTA; uses B-roll (works best with images/video matching what you say); good for profile visits; keep under 30 sec.
  - Best overall = a mix of all three.
- **Steal:** map each format to the *goal* it serves before you script.

### 7. Talking Head vs. Yaps
👀3,698 · 🔖123 · ↗️23
- **Format:** side-by-side format definition.
- **Her play:** Talking head = scripted, has cuts, tripod. Yap = structured but not scripted, minimal/no cuts, handheld. Two yap sub-types: (1) hot-take/opinion/advice (15-30s, off-the-cuff, simple hook), (2) structured lister (30-45s, phone set down, bullet-planned, often a notebook/slideshow/finger-counting, hook can be simple or heavily branded).
- **Steal:** the yap is the low-lift daily format; the talking head is the polished one. Know which you're filming before you start.

### 8. Ranking Viral Video Types
👀5,366 · 🔖117 · ↗️30
- **Format:** ranked tier list (1 = best for a founder).
- **Her ranking + reasoning:**
  1. **Yap** — highest-quality views; low effort but the take has to be genuinely valuable/personal. Advice: founders should whip out the phone and film a thought immediately, no over-planning.
  2. **Storytelling** — extremely personal, people fall in love with you and follow *you*.
  3. **Educational** — valuable, earns follows + leads.
  5. **Opinion** — risks rage-bait; can pull views but lower-quality (few leads/followers). She cites her own "got engaged at 16 / married at 19" videos: big views, weak leads.
  6. **Trends / memes** — nothing personal about you, so few followers or leads.
- **Steal:** rank *by lead quality*, not by views. High views ≠ good views.

### 9. 3 Types of Research
👀4,365
- **Format:** numbered method + cadence.
- **Her play:** (1) **Creator research** — find viral outliers from small/mid creators in-niche; batch ~1x/month. (2) **Keyword research** — search your niche/topic in the IG search tab, study the top performers; ~1-2x/month. (3) **Explore-page / Reels-recommendation research** — after doing the first two, the algorithm feeds you fresh in-niche outliers; a few times a week. Then take the viral **hooks and topics** and rebuild them with your own value/experience/stories so they stay authentic but proven. Source map: written+verbal hooks → keyword research; visual hooks + formats → reels tab / explore; topics → creator or keyword research.
- **Steal:** the cadence (monthly deep pulls, weekly light scan) and "reuse the hook, replace the substance."

### 10. Do Hashtags Even Work Anymore on Instagram?
👀2,742 · 🔖34
- **Format:** myth-bust, short answer / long answer.
- **Her play:** short answer, no — hashtags won't make you go viral. Long answer, they can act as keywords (niche, audience, topic), but your transcript + caption already carry those keywords. Bottom line: a good video is what makes you go viral; keywords, trending audio, or crazy editing won't rescue a bad one.
- **Steal:** the "short answer / long answer" structure for any myth question.

### 11. Is Educational Content Dead?
👀2,686 · 🔖24
- **Format:** contrarian myth-bust, personal proof.
- **Her play:** rejects the "you *must* post yaps / you *can't* post educational" absolutism. She's seen brands grow every possible way. Proof: she started a new page on her faith, posts mostly educational (Bible/Jesus), and it grows because people who care about the topic follow. Educational, authority/social-proof, and storytelling content are all alive; posting all three is ideal.
- **Steal:** answer a "is X dead?" question with a live counter-example of your own.

### 12. Personal Branding 101 (How to Never Destroy Your Reputation)
👀4,230 · 💬9 (her highest comment count in the set)
- **Format:** conviction/opinion monologue built on an extended analogy. Hook: "I bet you've never heard this advice before… I've never shared this publicly."
- **Her play:** analogizes "prosperity gospel vs true gospel" to "get-rich-quick creator advice vs the boring, hard, honest truth." Big churches teaching the easy message vs good pastors who build slowly and keep the most loyal members. Same with brands: honest, slow, convicting content takes ~10x longer but earns the most loyal know-like-trust audience. You'll get hate either way; do the right thing so you can live with yourself.
- **Steal:** the "you've never heard this / I've never said this publicly" hook, and using a personal-values analogy to make a branding point (high comments = polarizing on purpose).

### 13. Things I Find Incredibly Chic - Personal Brand Edition
👀3,097 · 🔖67
- **Format:** aspirational listicle (9 "green flags"), reframes advice as taste.
- **Her list (personal-branding green flags):** (1) not replying to hate, (2) a consistent editing style (fonts/brand colors so you'd know it was them without the face), (3) an even mix of educational/storytelling/structured + some yaps ("spontaneous but intentional"), (4) a repeatedly-stated overarching mission/goal to root for, (5) no fluffy trends, every post has a measurable takeaway, (6) visible effort/thought per video (PowerPoint, notebook, many shots, long-timeline projects), (7) storytelling videos loaded with matching B-roll, (8) clearly not copying anyone, (9) consistent posting / sticking to a schedule, posting like they have 100K even at 100 followers.
- **Steal:** turn a best-practices checklist into a "things I find chic" list. Same info, more aspirational, more shareable.

### 14. 100 View vs. 1M View Video
👀5,111
- **Format:** before/after comparison, teaching is on-screen text.
- **Her play:** a 1M-view video has all six ingredients dialed: **written hook + verbal hook + visual hook + script + format + CTA.** A 100-view video is missing/weak on these.
- **Steal:** the 6-ingredient checklist as a pre-post QA pass.

### 15-16. Weekly Diary - Episodes #19 & #20
#20: 👀2,841 · 🔖7 · ↗️2 · #19: 👀2,481 — **lowest saves/shares of the set.**
- **Format:** personal vlog, voiceover over B-roll of her day (painting the deck, husband Ben, Sunday morning).
- **Her play:** pure storytelling/relationship-building. Low saves by design; this feeds parasocial connection and loyalty, not reach.
- **Steal:** a numbered diary series builds the know-like-trust layer, but don't expect it to grow the account. Use it to *keep* an audience, not win one.

## The patterns worth naming (each traceable to the videos above)

- **Saves are her scoreboard.** Her three biggest videos by views (POV hire me 24.5K, Using AI 8,099, What Type of Content 6,354) are all "save-me-a-system" videos with heavy save counts. Her diaries (lowest saves) are her lowest reach.
- **Her repeatable hook formulas:** "If you had [time/constraint] to [outcome], how would you do it?" · "[X] vs [Y]" · "Ranking [category]" · "Is [thing] dead?" · "POV: you hire me as your ___" · "I bet you've never heard this…" · "Things I find incredibly chic."
- **Her content taxonomy (use as your own pillars):** Educational (saves/leads) · Storytelling (loyalty/profile visits) · Yaps (fast, ad-able, new-account fuel) · plus Diary (retention). Rank by lead quality, not views.
- **Her research → post loop:** find a proven outlier → keep the hook + topic → replace the substance with your own value/experience → template it in a Google Doc for reuse.

## Social to-do list for Luxury Catalog (for your review)

Framed against our current goal: **follows now** (quiz comes later). Saves + shares drive the reach that earns follows, so most of these target save/share rate.

### Do first (Recommended)
1. **Copy her cover system.** Lock one on-brand cover template: a single topic word in a bold brand color over a consistent frame, written hook baked in. *Moves: click-through → views, the top of the whole funnel.*
2. **Build a "save-me system" video, our version of "POV: you hire me."** Turn one Luxury Catalog process into a numbered walk-through (e.g. "POV: you ask me to authenticate your bag" or "how I price a bag in 6 steps"). *Moves: saves → reach → follows.*
3. **Adopt her 4 pillars for our calendar:** Educational (data/authentication/value) · Storytelling (founder + real bags) · Yaps (fast hot-takes to camera) · Diary (behind-the-scenes retention). Tag every planned post with one pillar + the goal it serves. *Moves: consistency + follow rate.*

### Do next
4. **Steal her hook formulas into our voice.** Draft 10 hooks using her templates against our topics ("Is the Speedy overrated?" as "Is [bag] dead?", "$300 bag vs $3,000 bag", "Ranking the most overrated it-bags"). *Moves: watch-time + shares.*
5. **Run her 3-types research loop weekly.** Monthly: batch creator + keyword research in the handbag/luxury niche for proven outlier hooks. Weekly: light explore-tab scan. Log hooks to reuse (this fits our existing trends pipeline). *Moves: hit rate per post.*
6. **Make a "100 vs 1M view" QA checklist** for every post before it ships: written hook, verbal hook, visual hook, script, format, CTA. *Moves: floor-raises every post.*
7. **Ship a "things I find incredibly chic about a luxury bag" listicle** (reframe a buying/authentication checklist as taste). *Moves: saves + shares.*

### Test, lower priority
8. **Try one myth-bust in her "short answer / long answer" format** ("Do you need the box and receipt to resell?"). *Moves: comments + saves.*
9. **Start a light numbered diary/BTS series** (sourcing, unboxing, a day building the site). Retention play, not a growth play, so cap the effort. *Moves: repeat views / loyalty, not reach.*
10. **A/B the yap format:** one polished talking-head vs one handheld off-the-cuff hot-take on the same topic, compare save + share rate. *Moves: tells us which production level earns our audience.*
