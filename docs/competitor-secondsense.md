## TL;DR
A funded competitor is live on our core idea. **SECONDSENSE is a price-comparison / "price index" engine** for secondhand luxury handbags. It is NOT a knowledge/taste/authentication/archive product. **Decision: differentiate against them on depth (authentication + production-canon archive + taste/identity + authored voice/GEO); do not match them on price aggregation.** The "Second Sense" name is now theirs and off the table for us.

## What they are (high-confidence, recurs across sources)
- Product: cross-site search that shows one unified view of what a specific bag is priced at across resale sites, vs a computed "market average." Serves "the decided shopper."
- Taglines: "Every listing. One view. Total confidence." / "the operating system for secondhand luxury" / "the authority resale price index."
- Funding: $2M oversubscribed, led by Outlander VC (Atlanta seed fund), ~Sept 30 2025.
- Founder/CEO: Chris Lucas (ex-Meta/Uber + HBS per a single snippet, unconfirmed). SF. Small early team.
- Breakout: Alix Earle organically shared it to 7M+ TikTok followers (April 2025). IG @secondsenseco ~8k followers.
- Monetization: undisclosed; consumer product free; almost certainly affiliate referral (inference; same rails as us).
