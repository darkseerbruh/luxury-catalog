**[OPEN — verbatim]**

Sit down, class is in session. I'm Arielle, founder of luxurycatalog.com, your resource for all the info on handbags. I'm going to teach you what to look for at the thrift by showing you what I look for.

**[LESSON 1 — the ones actually worth grabbing]**

Lesson one, the bags actually worth grabbing. This black **Guess tote** is a real brand, real everyday bag, and the little triangle on the zipper pull is how you know it's theirs. This **Steve Madden** in the yellow floral, flip it open, the name is printed right into the lining, that's a good sign it's really the brand and not a copy. **Vera Bradley** you can clock from across the store, the pattern is quilted into the cotton and the name is tucked right into the print, this one looks like their Ribbons print. And this camel **BCBG**, structured, clean lining, a real contemporary label that holds its shape. None of these are grails, but they're real bags that will actually last you. Resale on stuff like this is small, think ten to thirty-five dollars, so you buy them to carry, not to flip. That's a July twenty twenty-six estimate from sold comps, a range, not an appraisal.

**[PRICE BEAT — the honest cheap one]**

This canvas tote has no name in it, but it's **four ninety-nine**, good weight, tight stitching, and it is not going to come apart on you. Good bag, fair price, easy yes.

**[LESSON 2 — the laughable dupes]**

Okay, comedy hour. This one wants to be a Chanel so badly. It took the quilting, it took the chain, it took the little twist lock, it took every code except the one thing they cannot fake, **the real one**'s actual name. And it's made out of, what, a bath mat? A real Chanel flap is lambskin and costs more than my car. Here are your tells: no maker's stamp anywhere inside, threads already coming loose, and that **white made-in-China tag** flapping around. A real house does not tag a bag like a phone charger. Same joke with this woven mustard one, it's doing its best Bottega impression, but it's plastic and there's no logo, because there's no house behind it. And these **do not resell**, that's not me gatekeeping, it's just that nobody's out here shopping for a no-name knockoff.

**[LESSON 3 — the landfill pile]**

And then the landfill pile. This nude croc one photographs fine. Now **watch the handle**. See that? The coating is already cracking and peeling, and once PU starts to go it does not stop, you can't fix it. This is the stuff that's basically Shein with a thrift markup. Do the cost per wear, if it falls apart in a month, five dollars wasn't cheap, it was five dollars you set on fire.

**[CLOSE — verbatim]**

Ultimately we didn't find any grails today, but we did learn how to spot quality a little better. K thanks bye, see you next time.
