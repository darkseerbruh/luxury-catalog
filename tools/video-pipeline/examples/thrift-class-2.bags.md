## Notes / honesty flags for you
- **Only $4.99 is a hard number.** Every other Goodwill tag on screen is either the
  new QR style (no printed price) or too obscured to read. I did NOT invent asks.
  If you can read a price on any of these from your own footage, tell me and I'll
  add the callout.
- The **$4.99** tag hangs in the 7929 cluster near the beige canvas tote. Confirm it's
  that tote's tag before posting (you were there); if it belongs to a neighbor bag,
  say so and I'll move or cut the card.
- **7923/7924/7925 are one bag.** The white made-in-China / man-made-material care
  tag (7925) is the textbook "white tag, no maker's stamp" tell, so it anchors the
  dupe segment even though the bag isn't a Chanel copy per se.
- **7932 is the hero dupe:** it took every Chanel-flap code except the name, in fuzzy
  fleece. Best "took the codes, not the logo" example in the haul.
- No grails today. Best finds are real contemporary brands (Guess, Steve Madden,
  Vera Bradley, BCBG) — bags to carry, not to flip. Close stays "no grails."
